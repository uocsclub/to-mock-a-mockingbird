* Day-Knights and Night-Knights

The strange city of Subterranea is completely underground, and all timepieces are strictly forbidden. Yet the inhabitants have an uncanny sense of time; they always know when it is day and when it is night.

Each inhabitant either a day-knight or a night-knights (hard to type). The day-knights tell the truth during the day and lie during the night; the night-knights tell the truth during the night and lie during the day.

Suppose you meet an inhabitant and are allowed to ask him as many yes/no questions as you like. What is the minimum number of questions you need ask to find out whether it is day or night?

** Answer

Just one: "are you a day-knight?"

During the day, every inhabitant will claim to be a day-knight; during the knight, they will all claim to be night-knights.

* Who are you?

Suppose that you instead wish to know if the inhabitant to whom you are speaking is a day-knight or a night-knight. What is the smallest number of yes/no questions you need ask?

** Answer

Again, just one! "Is it daytime?"

Day-knights always claim it's the day, while night-knights always claim it's nighttime.

* Who are you part 2?

I came across an inhabitant who made a statement. Before he spoke, I did not know whether he was a day-knight or a night-knight, or whether it was day or night. After he made the statement, I knew that he was a day-knight and that it was night. Can you supply such a statement?

** Answer

"It is day and I'm a night-knight." Compare "I am a wealthy knave" from the last chapter.

This also combines are previous two insights: during the daytime, everyone claims to be a day-night and vice versa; and day-nights always claim it's the day, and vice versa.

* Who are you part 3?

On another occasion, I came across an inhabitant who made a statement from which I could deduce that he was a day-knight and that it was then day. What statement would work?

** Answer

"It is day or I'm a day-night."

If the statement is true, then the speaker is a day-night and it's day. If the statement is false, it is night and the speaker is a night-knight: but then it would have to be true!
