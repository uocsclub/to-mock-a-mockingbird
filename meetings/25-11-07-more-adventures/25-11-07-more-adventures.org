* The Next Day

Reminder: all inhabitants of the island are either knights, who always tell the truth; or knaves, who always lie.

I passed a native and asked him: "Do you ever answer no to questions?" He answered me with yes/no, and I then knew for sure whether he was a knight or a knave. Which was he?

** Answer

If he answered no, he would clearly be lying and a knave. Thus, he's a knave. (see commentary)

* The Sociologist

On the next day, I met a sociologist who was visiting the island. He gave me the following report:

"I have interviewed all the natives of this island and I have observed a curious thing: For every native X, there is at least one native Y such that Y claims that X and Y are both knaves."

Does this report hold water?

** Answer

About as much water as a colander! Clearly, Y can't be a knight, so Y must be a knave. This means that X must be a knight. But X is every native, and we've shown there's at least one knave. Contradiction!

* My Last Adventure

I met a native who said, "This is not the first time I have said what I am now saying." Was the native a knight or a knave?

** Answer

The native must be a knave! If he was a knight, he could have never said it for the first time. (And incidentally, the knave will never be able to say this statement ever again.)
